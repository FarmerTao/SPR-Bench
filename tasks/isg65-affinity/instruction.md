# ISG65: apparent steady-state affinity

Fit the six observed ISG65 concentration-response series (C3b/C3d × WT/ΔL2/L2N188A) using the measured end-of-association responses. Report residuals and coverage; do not treat the fit as a kinetic mechanism.

Measured data are in `/app/data`. Read `/app/spec/CONTRACT.md` and implement the reproducible analysis program. No narrative report is graded. Input responses are experimental; no simulated curves are included. Identifiers may be aliased in the robustness subset; do not key analysis off names.
